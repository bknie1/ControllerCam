## 1.5.5

- **The More menu (Y) shows every action for an item,** in your bags and at the bank: Use or Equip first, then the move (To bank, To bags, Sell...), Equip by name for gear at the bank, Pick up, Split, Mark, Item details, Try on and Destroy.
- **Buy a bank bag slot** from the More menu at the bank, with its price, asking first.
- **Bank bags:** the bank's bag list no longer includes one of your own bag slots (it showed as an extra bag and inflated the counts). The debug report lists the bank's containers.
- **Ammo, herb and crafting bags** come after your ordinary bags, and the keyring last.
- **Custom Sets in the transmogrifier:** Y deletes the set your cursor is on, after asking.
