## 1.4.5

- **Upgrades by spec:** an item's tooltip and the Market say which of your specs it's an upgrade for ("Upgrade: Holy", or "Upgrade: Retribution (other spec)"). Gear is weighed for each spec: a Holy paladin by intellect, Protection by stamina, Retribution by strength, and so on. A setting picks which specs count: **Current spec only**, **Primary and secondary** (the default) or **Any spec** (`/cchud upgradespecs`). Gear only your other spec could use is no longer sold by your junk rules.
- **The Mentor:** the profession levelling plan talks you through it in plain words ("You've outgrown your recipes. See a trainer: Smelt Mithril is waiting at 175."), its lines no longer overlap, and it sits clear of the profession window's tabs.
