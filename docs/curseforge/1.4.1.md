## 1.4.1

### The CCHUD stack
- **ControllerCam: InputSwap** now comes in the same download, beside ControllerCam and ControllerCam: HUD: the Gamepad UI switches on and off by whether you last moved with the stick or the keyboard. Its settings are kept.

### Camera
- **Closer on an enemy target** (off until you set it: `/ccam targetzoom 6`, or the slider in the options): target an enemy and the camera comes in close; let it go and it goes back out to where you had it.
- **Underwater**, the camera no longer tilts with the sea bed.

### HUD
- **Big moments** show the burst's total, the abilities listed beside it; small crits are left out.
- **Breath with bubbles** beside the vertical bars when you swim (needs a full restart of the game after updating).
- **Your controller's buttons** in the HUD's prompts: white A B X Y on black for a Steam controller, coloured letters for Xbox, coloured shapes for PlayStation (full restart).
- **Fishing:** X hooks the fish and A casts again; nothing is taken while a window is open, and B is yours unless there are weapons to take back.
- **Quests:** your focused quest stays yours; an option focuses the nearest quest.
- **Fixed:** nameplates flashing in and out and shaking, the tooltip's vendor lines flickering.
