## 1.4.9

- **One bag window, made for the controller:** all your items in one grid, empty slots gathered at the end, your bags in a row of their own, LB/RB quick filters, and the bank in the same window (`/cchud bagwindow`).
- **A quest compass** along the top of the screen (or the bottom, swapping with the XP bar): quest objectives and turn-ins at their bearing with their distance, fading in and out as you turn (`/cchud compass`).
- **Cinematic flights:** letterbox bars and a slow, wide camera on you and your mount while you ride, easing out as you come in to land (`/cchud flightscene`).
- **A healer's party and raid:** in a healing spec, your party sits beside your character with each member's target button, and the raid look is slimmer. Each frame shows only who needs healing, a debuff you can cure, and whether they're in range (`/cchud healerframes`).
- **Sell unless there's a reason not to:** items say sell, unless they're an upgrade for one of your characters ("Upgrade for Sol"), an enchanter of yours would rather disenchant them, or a quest you can still do wants them: "[37] Bloodscalp Insight in Stranglethorn Vale". Unbound upgrades also say what they'd fetch at the auction house.
- **Nameplates your way:** Small/Normal/Large bars, full, short or no names, and a one-press look: Full, Minimal or Minimal mini (`/cchud platesize`, `platename`, `platelook`).
