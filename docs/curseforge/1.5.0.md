## 1.5.0

- **The HUD in modules: parts you switch off cost nothing.** Twelve parts of the HUD are now addons of their own, loaded only while they're switched on: nameplates, party and raid frames, numbers and callouts, the bag window, the Market, The Mentor, transmog sets, the compass, fishing, the characters in the windows, the campfire, and flights.
  - **Where to switch them:** Options > Modules.
  - **Switching on during play:** a part loads at once, after a fight if you're in one.
  - **Switching off:** frees its memory at your next /reload.
  - **The AddOns list:** a module you untick there stays off. The modules sit under the HUD, and the HUD under ControllerCam.
- **Less memory:** the HUD keeps only your language's texts, about 1.5 MB less.

Everything comes in the same one download.
