## 1.5.2

- **Enemy class colours in battlegrounds:** the other side's health bars try their class colour again (a Priest's white, a Mage's blue). If the game refuses, the bar keeps its usual colour, and the HUD doesn't try again.
- **The battleground start timer** now sits below the target frame instead of over it.
- **The spirit healer's countdown stays in sight:** while you're a ghost in a battleground, "Resurrection in 12 s" stays at the top of the screen, and no button closes it. If the countdown was cancelled, it tells you to talk to the spirit healer.
