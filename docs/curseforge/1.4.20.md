## 1.4.20

- **Your movement settings are yours.** The camera profiles used to set the game's controller movement options as well: facing the way you move, turning with the camera, and the stick's turn and look speeds. Now they leave those alone until you say they may.
  - **Asked once:** at your first login after updating, ControllerCam asks (A: let the profiles set them, B: keep mine).
  - **Keep mine:** puts back anything a profile had already changed, as you had it.
  - **Changing it later:** use the new option **Profiles also set how you move and the stick's speeds**, or `/ccam movement`.
