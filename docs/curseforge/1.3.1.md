## 1.3.1

### The Broker and the Market
- **The Broker speaks for himself** at the top of the auction house's Market: what is worth selling, what more could be with patience, how fresh his prices are, in his own words.
- **Chance to sell, honestly:** each row ends with his word on it, "I think it'll sell (75%)", "A bit of a gamble (40%)", "I wouldn't hold my breath (15%)". The estimate now weighs your price against the lowest and the usual, how many are up, where prices are heading, how your own auctions of it went and how fresh the prices are; the item's pane says what moved it.
- **One row of buttons**, each action once, with your controller's own letters (A B X Y).
- **The price chart** is green where the price rose and red where it fell, or a week of daily candles (R3).

### The Postmaster
- The mailbox speaks in chat as **[Postmaster]**, in his own words.
- **The subject from what you attach:** attaching items fills in an empty subject ("Linen Cloth x20", or the first and how many more). A subject you typed is left alone.

### Fixes
- Putting the fishing pole away no longer leaves the gamepad bars hidden or the cast prompt up.
- The mailbox's Take everything button no longer runs past the window: it sits under it, beside the tabs.
- Options: in a row of choices, the chosen button is plain to see and the others are dimmed.
