## 1.4.0

### A game on the way
- On a flight, a line under the flight bar offers a **small game** (needs a full restart of the game after updating, not just /reload). Your mount flies through a lane of clouds: **A** flaps it up and it sinks again on its own, with wide gaps and slow clouds. **X** fires a net at the riders coming the other way: a rider you catch is grounded, never slain, and floats down under a parachute. Catches earn flight gold.
- **Y** is high altitude: the game is put away and A and X are the game's again. A fight or the landing gives every button back at once. Off with `/cchud flightgame off`.

### Quests
- **Your focused quest stays yours:** it was dropped whenever the quest list was put in order (right after closing the map). It is now put back after every change.
- **Focus the nearest quest** (option, off by default): the quest the game points you to is always the nearest one in your list as you travel; one you focus yourself is left alone for ten minutes.

### Big moments
- **Small crits are left out:** a crit well under what your crits usually do (a seal's proc beside a Holy Strike, a dot ticking) no longer starts or joins a burst.
