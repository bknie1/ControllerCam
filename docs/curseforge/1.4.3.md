## 1.4.3

- **The other faction, plainly:** a player of the other faction wears the faction's badge before the name and an outline round the health bar, Alliance blue or Horde red, on nameplates and the target frame. On RP-PvP and PvP realms, no mistaking them for a friend. Names keep their colours.
- **The campfire scene** starts while you read the fire's buff with the cursor out (it used to wait on you forever).
- **Hide the game's red error messages** (`/cchud quieterrors on`, off by default): "Spell is not ready yet." and the like stay out of sight.
- `/cchud momenttest` shows a big moment as one looks now.
