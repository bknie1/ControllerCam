## 1.4.10

- **Items are kept unless they're clearly junk:** only grey items and what you mark as junk are advised for sale. Gear for your other spec (a Protection sword and shield), anything in an equipment set, things you use (a Cozy Sleeping Bag) and unique items are kept, each with its reason. A setting brings back selling when unsure (`/cchud adviceunsure`).
- **Your bags work in a fight:** open, move, X to drink a potion or swap a weapon (armour is refused politely), B to close.
- **Destroy** from an item's More menu (Y), with a hold to confirm. Each bag shows its slots used of total.
- **Fixed: using an item from the bag window** no longer gets the addon blocked by the game. Items are used through the game's own secure button, and **X/Square is Use** (A picks up).
- **Fixed: at the mailbox, a vendor, the bank, a trade or the auction house** LT/RT move between that window and your bags again, and each window's own buttons work while you're in it.
- **Bags, smarter:** what you use comes first (consumables, items with a Use: effect), junk is faded at the end, quest items are marked, and your empty slots show as empty slots. A **By type** view groups everything into labelled sections (`/cchud bagorder`).
- **Cinematic flights circle slowly** round you and your mount, and the minimap's marks are hidden for the scene (and the campfire's).
- **The other faction on the target frame again:** badge and outline are back, and the health bar is lit in the faction's colour, or an enemy's red if you prefer (`/cchud factionstyle`).
- **Big moments are brief and read at a glance:** the abilities in one line with the hit count, the damage under them, gone in about as long as a crit's flash.
- **Seals call out as they run out every time,** also in a fight where the game hides a buff's time.
- **Fixed:** a Lua error with raid marks the game keeps secret.
