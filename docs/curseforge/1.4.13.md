## 1.4.13

- **Fixed: X at a vendor sells.** In the bag window, X with a vendor open sells the selected item, gear included (it used to equip a bind-on-equip piece), the game's own sale with buyback as usual.
- **Plain gear sells again:** white gear, and green bind-on-equip gear not yet bound, is advised for sale when it's below what you wear in that slot or not your armour type, as long as no enchanter of yours would skill up on it and nothing else says keep. It says why.
- **Fixed: moving things out of the bank.** At the bank, LT/RT and the back button switch the bag window between your bags and the bank, the controller stays with it, and X on a bank item moves it to your bags.
- **R3 sorts again** in the bag window (your bags, or the bank in the bank view); tooltips always show.
- **The Hearthstone comes first** in the bag window's Smart order.
