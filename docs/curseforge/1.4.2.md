## 1.4.2 (with 1.4.0 and 1.4.1)

### The CCHUD stack
- **ControllerCam: InputSwap** now comes in the same download, beside ControllerCam and ControllerCam: HUD: the Gamepad UI switches on and off by whether you last moved with the stick or the keyboard. Its settings are kept.

### Party and quests
- **Party chat hears only a finished quest** by default, marked as ours: `CCHUD: Bloody Bones: ready to hand in`. Your steps stay in your own chat (an option sends every step).
- **Finishing up:** when your party has finished a quest you are still on, you say so once: `CCHUD: Finishing up [Bloody Bones].`
- **Your focused quest stays yours** through the list being sorted; an option focuses the nearest quest.

### A game on the way
- On a flight, a small game under the flight bar: fly your mount through the clouds and net the riders coming the other way (needs a full restart of the game after updating).

### Camera
- **Closer on an enemy target** (off until you set it: `/ccam targetzoom 6`, or the slider in the options): target an enemy and the camera comes in close; let it go and it goes back out.
- **Underwater**, the camera no longer tilts with the sea bed.

### HUD
- **Big moments** show the burst's total, the abilities listed beside it; small crits are left out.
- **Breath with bubbles** beside the vertical bars when you swim (full restart).
- **Your controller's buttons** in the HUD's prompts: white A B X Y on black for a Steam controller, coloured letters for Xbox, coloured shapes for PlayStation (full restart).
- **Fishing:** X hooks the fish and A casts again; nothing is taken while a window is open.
- **Fixed:** nameplates flashing in and out and shaking, the tooltip's vendor lines flickering.
