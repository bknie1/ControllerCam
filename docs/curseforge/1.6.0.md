## 1.6.0

- **Hits and casts, animated.** One new choice, **Hit and cast effects**: Full (the default), Subtle, Classic (as it was) or None.
  - **Hits land somewhere:** swings and your named abilities strike at one of eight places on a health bar, and spells come in from an end of it.
  - **Wounds stay open:** a gash below half health, two more below a quarter. The living bleed, elementals crumble, the undead leak.
  - **Bolts charge up and fire:** Fireball, Frostbolt, Shadow Bolt and the rest gather at the end of your cast bar and go off with a jolt. Wrath, Aimed Shot, Arcane Missiles, Mind Flay and Drain Life have casts of their own.
  - **The cast time grows** over a cast's last second and bursts as it lands, and **casts in a row are counted** (x2, x3...).
- **The recommended camera, tuned by zoom:** shoulder offset, pitch and target focus change with how far you zoom, plus a **Shoulder offset strength** slider.
- **Native is law:** the game's own bags and bank are back, with our additions on them: hold R3 on an item for its actions, the Materials list works with the controller.
- **The Mentor** plans for levelling on your own: 81 Cooking and 12 First Aid recipes, what to farm, buy or bid on, and which recipes make money.
- **Your character has more to say:** about fish, treasure, dungeon bosses and the road, each people in its own voice.
- **The flight game, grown up:** larger, with upgrades bought from flight gold. The fishing game is offered the same way.
- **Chat:** who's healing you shows on your health bar; short channel names, ads shown once, names as First L., text sized for a TV.
- Many fixes: buffs wearing off no longer flash as a fight starts, fishing counts, tooltips, transmog sets, the stance and pet wheels.
