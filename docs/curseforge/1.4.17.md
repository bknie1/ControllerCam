## 1.4.17

- **Characters in the windows.** Every window the HUD improves now has someone who helps you, with a portrait and one short line about what's in front of you and why:
  - over your bags, you, in your race's way of speaking
  - the vendor, the banker and the Postmaster
  - the flight master
  - the Broker at the auction house
  - The Mentor at your profession

  One switch turns them all off: **Narrative: characters in the windows**.
- **A Sets tab at the transmogrifier.** Gear that shares a name (Green Iron Helm, Green Iron Gauntlets... make **Green Iron**) and the game's own sets go on your outfit in one press. **Undo** puts the slots back. Nothing is paid for until you press the game's **Apply**.
- **Appearance in item tooltips:** collected, collected from another item, or not collected yet.
- **The Mentor follows the levelling guides.** Each stretch of skill uses the recipe Wowhead's guides use, with alternatives and why you might pick one. Recipes you don't know yet are marked to learn.
  - **With the bank** lists what you can make once your bank is counted.
  - L3 puts the panel away and brings it back.
  - RB tries the recipe's gear on.
  - The title no longer gets cut off.
- **Try on** in the bag window's More menu shows gear on you in the dressing room.
- **Options laid out by topic.** Each page has headings: Your frames, Target, party and raid, Nameplates, Effects, Vibration, Camera and aiming, and more. The aiming dot is under Camera and aiming.
- **Every part of the HUD has a switch** in the Modules list on the main options page.
- **Fixed:** vendor item tooltips disappeared whenever your bags redrew.
- **Fixed:** at the bank, LT/RT left the bag window's cursor stuck. The bag window now follows the game's focus to its bank window and back.
