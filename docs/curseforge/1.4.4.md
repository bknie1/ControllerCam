## 1.4.4

New name: **ControllerCam + HUD (CCHUD) for Gamepad**. Same folders, settings and commands.

- **Sell some, keep the rest:** the Market posts as many as you choose. D-pad left and right set the count ("5 of 7"), and the Broker says what you let go and what stays with you.
- **Fewer things kept that sell:** quest items no quest of yours needs (Green Hills of Stranglethorn pages, Gorilla Fangs, Lesser Bloodstone Ore) and spare potions and drinks go to the auction house when it pays clearly more than a vendor.
- **Recipes count your bank:** the profession window shows a second count with what's in your bank, e.g. [20] [40].
- **Flights:** an early landing sends the bar to the next stop, and on arrival chat gives the time of day and the air of the place ("Evening at Booty Bay: hot and humid, the air thick enough to drink").
- **The flight game** is set in the sky (treetops and zeppelins), taller, with how-to-play at the start, and plays over your mount so zone names stay in sight.
- **Party frames** follow the party when someone leaves or is kicked.
- **Nameplates no longer flash** when several enemies move behind walls and trees.
- **"Spell is not ready yet." and the like are hidden** (`/cchud notready`); other red messages still show.
- **The other faction's badge** is bigger, beside the health bar.
