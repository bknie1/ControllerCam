## 1.4.19

- **The bank is in the bag window.** The bank sits on the left and your bags on the right, both drawn the same way, and the controller starts there.
  - **Moving around:** LT goes to the bank and RT to your bags.
  - **Bank items work like your bags:** X moves an item across, Y opens the menu, A picks it up.
  - **Closing** the window closes the bank.
  - **Turning it off:** switch off **One bag window** to get the game's own bank window back.
- **Quick Deposit and Quick Withdraw,** plus **Deposit materials** and **Withdraw materials** by kind (Metal & Stone, Cloth, Herb...) in the Y menu at the bank.
- **A calmer bag window:**
  - It keeps one height whatever filter you choose, and fills from the top down.
  - The buttons' line sits in a tab below it.
  - Tooltips and gear comparisons stay outside the window instead of covering your items.
- **Crafting value in item advice:**
  - It keeps what your professions will need next, and says when ("Blacksmithing needs it from 235").
  - It compares selling a material as it is with crafting it first ("Craft into Golden Rod: 94s each vs 42s as it is").
  - It shows what a crafted item's materials cost.
- **Names and nameplates leave the picture** on the cinematic flight, at the campfire and while you're AFK, and come back afterwards.
