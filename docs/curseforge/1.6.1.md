## 1.6.1

- **Each form and stance its own actions.** The game's gamepad bar has a Stance Bar: eight actions for each form, which take the place of one cluster of the bar while you're in it. The game does the switching, in a fight too. Now you can use it:
  - **Choose the cluster** under *Gamepad bars*: Off, Left trigger (the start), Right trigger or Both triggers.
  - **A form's bar fills itself** the first time you shift into it: Claw, Rake and Shred in cat form, Maul and Growl in bear, Taunt and Shield Block in defensive stance. What is on it already stays.
- **A finished quest is a link:** party chat says `[CCHUD] [Bloody Bones] completed`, and the name can be clicked.
- **Gathering and crafting on the cast bar:** herbs are plucked leaf by leaf, the bar knows the herb or vein you work on, and a craft shows the item you're making.
- **The AddOns list is tidy:** the HUD and its modules sit under *ControllerCam for Gamepad*.
- Fixes: an error from the stance and pet buttons, and the Reload UI button sits clear of the game's prompt.
