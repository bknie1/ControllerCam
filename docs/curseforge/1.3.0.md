## 1.3.0

### Screen fade, the most asked for
- The screen's fade out of combat has its own options page (**Screen fade**): the fade itself, how faint, and each part on its own line (minimap, quest list, gamepad bars, damage meter, experience bar, buffs, cooldown rows). Turn one part off and it stays put while the rest fades. By command: `/cchud fade`, `/cchud fade minimap off`, `/cchud fade all off`.
- Opening the world map no longer holds the game up: the quest list is never re-sorted while the map is open.

### ControllerCam
- The game's notice about its experimental camera settings is asked once, not at every login.
- Fishing has a camera of its own: close over your shoulder, looking out at the float.

### ControllerCam: HUD
- **Flights:** where to and how long in chat, a bar that fills on the way with your gryphon, wind rider or bat gliding along it; each route's time is learned.
- **Mailbox:** Take everything, for batches of auction sales.
- **Big moments:** crits landing back to back are pinned with their total, the abilities listed beside it.
- **Cooldown callouts:** your key abilities call out as they come back, instead of a bulky cooldown bar.
- **Reticle styles** for the aiming dot.
- **Campfire scene:** sit at a camp and the screen turns to film, retelling your big moments and your camp's gifts.
- **Wounds:** hit effects by the kind of foe, building as it weakens; an execute-range glow at 25% or less.
- **Frames:** enemy power bar on the target frame, heal previews on party frames (yours and others'), raid target marks, friendly NPCs coloured by reputation, a vertical cast bar option, a sleepy player frame in inns and cities.
- **Fishing:** one press to cast again, the minigame played on the cast bar, the prompt out of the way while bait goes on.
- **Smart sell:** a confirmation listing what is sold and what is kept; gear judged by stat weights for your class; your last crafting tool of a kind is kept.
- **Chat:** first names only, as an option.
- **Steam Controller:** A, B, X and Y button letters.
- Many fixes, among them enemy cast bars that turned solid white.
