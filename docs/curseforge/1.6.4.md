## 1.6.4

New in 1.6.4: the HUD folder carries its licence file; nothing changes in the game.

The 1.6 releases so far, with the big things first.

- **Each form and stance its own actions.** Bear, cat, defensive stance, berserker stance and stealth each get eight actions of their own on the gamepad bar, through the game's own Stance Bar. The game does the switching as you shift, in a fight too.
  - **Choose which cluster they take** under *Gamepad bars*: Off, Left trigger (the start), Right trigger or Both triggers.
  - **A form's bar fills itself** the first time you shift into it: Claw, Rake and Shred in cat form, Maul and Growl in bear, Taunt and Shield Block in defensive stance. What is on it already stays.
- **Hits and casts, animated.** One choice, **Hit and cast effects**, sets how much you see: Full (the start), Subtle, Classic or None.
  - **Hits land somewhere:** swings and your named abilities strike at one of eight places on a health bar, and wounds open and stay open as a foe weakens.
  - **Bolts charge up and fire:** Fireball, Frostbolt, Shadow Bolt and the rest gather at the end of your cast bar and go off with a jolt. Wrath, Aimed Shot, Arcane Missiles, Mind Flay and Drain Life have casts of their own.
  - **The cast time grows** over a cast's last second, and **casts in a row are counted** (x2, x3...), with the damage you dealt while the chain ran beside the count (1.4k).
  - **The chain's count wears the spell** (new in 1.6.3): the spell's own effect glows and rains behind the count, stronger with every cast up to x25, and a long chain's count grows.
  - **The spell's name is under the cast bar** (new in 1.6.3), out of the way of what plays on the bar; the chain's count sits to the right of the cast time.
  - **What helps your cast shows over the bar** (new in 1.6.3): up to three icons for the effects on you that make this cast faster, free or stronger, with how many are left.
- **Gathering and crafting on the cast bar.**
  - **A herb grows as the kind of plant it is** (new in 1.6.3): flowers on a stem in the herb's own colour, Earthroot and Liferoot with their roots under the bar, Stranglekelp swaying with bubbles, leafy herbs in their own green. When the cast lands, the plants are plucked one by one.
  - **A knife for skinning** (new in 1.6.3) works at the cut as the hide lifts.
  - **A vein takes its metal's colour**, from copper to thorium.
  - **A craft shows the item you're making**, faint at first and whole when it's made.
- **The Market:** at the auction house, the item you chose now shows its own tooltip beside the price chart and the Broker's numbers, so you see what it is and what it's worth in one place.
- **Professions, with The Mentor:** a plan for levelling on your own, with 81 Cooking and 12 First Aid recipes, what to farm, buy or bid on, and which recipes make money. Its Alchemy plan now follows this game, where healing potions belong to First Aid.
- **The recommended camera, tuned by zoom:** shoulder offset, pitch and target focus change with how far you zoom.
- **A finished quest is a link:** party chat says `[CCHUD] [Bloody Bones] completed`, and the name can be clicked.
- **The AddOns list is tidy:** the HUD and its modules sit under *ControllerCam for Gamepad*.
- Fixes: an error from the stance and pet buttons, buffs wearing off no longer flash as a fight starts, and the Reload UI button sits clear of the game's prompt.
